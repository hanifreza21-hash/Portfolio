About me

HI! I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering Graduate and researcher dedicated to bridging the gap between traditional power systems and advanced computational intelligence.

My expertise spans Photovoltaics, smart grid resilience, renewable energy integration, and intelligent power electronics. With a proven track record of applying machine learning to complex materials sensing and power optimization challenges, I thrive on interdisciplinary innovation whether it is simulating advanced energy systems or deploying hardware solutions to solve real-world problems.

Recent highlight: Published in Journal of Hazardous Materials Advances (IF 7.7) on machine learning-driven terahertz PCF sensors for explosive detection.

Research Interests

Photovoltaics & Renewable Energy Integration

Modeling and simulation of solar PV systems, MPPT algorithm development, and integration of renewable sources into modern power grids for sustainable energy solutions.

Keywords: Solar PV, MPPT, P&O Technique, Renewable Energy, Grid Integration

Smart Grid & Distribution Network Resilience

Research on smart grid systems, V2G (Vehicle-to-Grid) technology integration, and distribution network reliability for next-generation power infrastructure.

Keywords: Smart Grid, V2G, EV Integration, Distribution Networks, Power Systems

Power Electronics & Multilevel Inverters

Harmonic distortion reduction in transformerless inverters, design of multilevel inverter topologies, and LCL filter optimization for improved power quality.

Keywords: Multilevel Inverters, Harmonic Reduction, LCL Filter, Power Quality

Machine Learning & AI Applications

Applying machine learning to engineering challenges, including terahertz PCF sensing for explosive detection and intelligent control systems for power electronics.

Keywords: Machine Learning, PCF Sensing, Terahertz, AI, Signal Processing
